Why I chose web development

I wanted to build websites that people can actually use. Web development lets me turn an idea into a page anyone can open in a browser.

Starting with the basics

Build small projects. Every page you finish teaches you more than an hour of watching tutorials.

MR. GOSLINE

What I learned so far

  • HTML5: the structure of a page.
  • CSS3: layout, colors, and responsive design.
  • JavaScript: tabs, buttons, and interactivity.
  • PHP and MySQL: forms and databases.
  • Git and GitHub: saving and sharing my code.

What comes next

I keep building projects, like my portfolio, a landing page, and a login page. Next, I want to learn more about backend development and publish my work online.
